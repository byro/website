Hosting of the website

byro.info is a static website. We do not use cookies or any analytics or tracking services. Fonts, images and scripts are delivered directly by this website.

If you select a language with the language switcher, your browser stores this choice locally (localStorage). It does not leave your device and only makes the start page forward you to this language on your next visit.

The website is provided via GitHub Pages. The provider is GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.

When you access a page, GitHub receives technically necessary connection data, in particular your IP address and the requested page. GitHub logs and stores IP addresses for security purposes.

The legal basis for our use of the hosting is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is to provide this website securely and reliably. You are not obliged by law or contract to provide the data. Without the technically necessary transmission, however, the website cannot be displayed.

Personal data may be processed in the USA in the process. According to its own statement, GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. For transfers covered by it, the adequacy decision of the European Commission applies.

GitHub determines the storage period based on the purpose of the processing and legal requirements. GitHub does not state a specific retention period for the IP logs of GitHub Pages in the linked information.

Further information can be found in GitHub’s privacy statement.
